NAAAN is a composer and multi-instrumentalist working across punk rock, progressive metal, techno, industrial rock, experimental music, black metal and alt rock. Across two decades this has meant guitar, bass, 6-string bass, vocals, synth and drum programming, spread across four very different bands.

Every project sounds different on purpose. What ties them together is the same restless approach to songwriting — heavy, electronic, melodic and unafraid to mix genres inside a single record.

"Different bands, different sounds — same obsession with pushing a song somewhere it isn't supposed to go."

This site brings every project together in one place, so you can listen, download and leave a comment on any release.
